# 01: Brief and scenario

The coordinator records the audience, channel, observable benefit, allowed final
address, style and audio choice in scenario Markdown outside Git. The supported
demo is silent. A real product requires authorized routes and assets in
[02](02-inputs-and-forbidden.md) before capture; do not infer access permission.

Keep the existing five-scene template. The synthetic configuration uses:

| Scene | Finished interval | Duration | Evidence/claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | 0–6s | 6s | A little board for a clearer day |
| Collect | 6–17s | 11s | Collect your next steps |
| Choose | 17–28s | 11s | Choose what matters today |
| Complete | 28–39s | 11s | Celebrate steady progress |
| Outro | 39–45s | 6s | Little Board; synthetic local demo |

For each screen caption name a recording, file-relative interval, action and
visible state that proves the claim. The demo uses intervals 1–12, 12–23 and
23–34 seconds of each recording. Keep caption lines short: the existing config
gate estimates wrapping at 45 characters, at most two lines. Actual font
wrapping must also pass independent visual review in every format.

Do not describe capabilities that the frame cannot establish. Rewrite an
unsupported claim or obtain an authorized capture plan for it. A config `note`
may explain omitted scope; it cannot excuse a false caption. The final name,
tagline and address must be accurate; the synthetic demo displays a local-demo
label, not an invented public destination.

The scenario includes the forbidden-content inventory, synthetic-data intervals,
font/asset provenance, chosen timings and any justified omissions. Configuration
is executable trusted code; the template validates a 45-second total and the
existing config gate exports the inspection TSV. Independent caption evidence
is completed after rendering, as specified in [07](07-final-check.md).

Proceed to [authorized inputs](02-inputs-and-forbidden.md). Use the existing
[synthetic config](../../../../demo/synthetic/config.mjs) as the minimum
accepted example; do not introduce another rendering engine.
